Solid Mechanics Columns

Euler's Critical Buckling Load

P_cr = (π² · E · I_min) / (L_eff)²
Both hinged: L_eff = L | Fixed/Free: L_eff = 2L

Valid only for long slender columns where buckling governs over crushing.

Soil Mechanics Consolidation

Terzaghi's 1-D Consolidation

T_v = (c_v · t) / (d)²
c_v = k / (m_v · γ_w)

d = drainage path (H/2 for two-way drainage, H for single drainage).

Manning's Formula & Hydraulic Jump

V = (1/n) · R^(2/3) · S^(1/2)
y2/y1 = (1/2) · [√(1 + 8·Fr1²) - 1]

Belanger's momentum equation for sequent depths in rectangular channels.

First-Order BOD Kinetics

BOD_t = L_0 · [1 - e^(-k · t)]
k_T = k_20 · (1.047)^(T - 20)

L_0 = Ultimate carbonaceous BOD; k = reaction rate constant.
